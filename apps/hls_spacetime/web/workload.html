How to read the geometry

The floor shows placement and highlights tiles with a known primitive output change at the selected clock. Lines show structural connections selected by that activity. A changed primitive can have several output bits. The replay samples after settling and cannot establish which pin toggled first or which path carried a transition.

Vivado height accumulates maximum SDF delays from a launch clock pin. Solid net lines pass through the recovered switch tiles, with height interpolated between the two timed pins. Dashed lines join endpoints where the checkpoint branch could not be resolved. Vertical strokes show individual primitive timing arcs.

openXC7 placement timing shows both ends of each modeled connection and the delay through each primitive. Horizontal position is the pin’s containing tile. Click a pin to see the other end, or choose Endpoint spans for all schematic connections. nextpnr estimates net delays from placement. Each axis fits its own implementation unless Shared ns scale is selected. Cell pairs retains the synthesis-level logic depth view.
