HLS SpacetimeSaved execution explorer
Space across each chip layer. Clock cycles along the stack.

This is a 3D scene: orbit changes the viewing direction, pan moves the view, and zoom changes its scale. Layer spacing separates clock cycles without changing their meaning. Opaque objects can hide others behind them; rotate the scene or change the clock window to see what is occluded. Top, Side and 3D restore useful camera directions. Expand scene gives the drawing the full window. The evidence inspector and flat chip panel are optional. Select a recorded operation, then follow its inputs or consumers.

Solid cubes mark recorded access positions, not known electrical switching. Distributed accesses follow associated native tile positions; cells sharing a tile can share a mark. Where a write mask identifies bits, the positions follow those selected bits. Read marks may represent whole-word context because the consumed bit subset is unknown: their solid shape does not mean the subset is known. Inspect the access mask and binding details for that distinction. Turning off Distributed accesses groups positions; a group centre is not the location of its computation. Show unmapped participants adds unknown-location context; its position outside the chip is a layout convention. Display filters do not remove operations or their evidence from the recording.

In Clock planes view, thicker storage links join a write at C to its Q sample at C+1. Thinner links show residence of the same version between samples; both are opaque in the 3D view. By default, only versions with recorded uses are drawn, ending at their last use. A hold or delayed response does not extend source-word use. Full storage history includes other residence. Hidden does not mean proven dead: unobserved consumers may exist. Selecting a word expands its qualified cells by default; the expansion control can group them again. Residence depends on the declared write coverage and does not establish native enables or exclusive occupancy. All glyph sizes and link thicknesses aid legibility; they are not physical cell dimensions or metal widths.

Computation circuits show the native combinational fan-in of recorded writes at their clocks, back to register outputs, constants and explicit boundary gaps. Function colors distinguish data, enables, resets and shared circuitry. Occupied tiles retains the spatial footprint; Wire routes adds retained electrical connections; Routes + switches also marks routing switches. Native wire tiles and PIP order locate the connection, but the drawn spans do not claim a measured metal trajectory. Mux alternatives remain structural context, not observed switching paths. Dim context uses muted colors, not transparency or an X-ray view.

Within-clock arrival places old Q sampled at C on the launch reference plane, combinational pins at their latest static arrival, and write C at the next Q/capture plane C+1. Auto STA uses the saved whole-design reference estimate; Original target uses its constraint, and Manual supplies a shared hypothetical period. Missing clock evidence falls back explicitly to the shared manual value, initially 10 ns. The spatial scale per nanosecond stays fixed across matched captures. None is a measured running clock. Late arrivals extend beyond the next reference boundary. Gray links distinguish other-input readiness and ready-to-capture intervals from recorded storage. Logic is shown at output settling time; red marks budget overruns. Geometry without arrival timing is omitted here; clock planes retain its spatial context. Known endpoints with incomplete route geometry stay connected by a thin core with a dashed sleeve. Heights between measured pin endpoints are interpolated along schematic routing chords. These are timing bounds, not a switching trace. Computation + urgency keeps destination hue and brightens complete paths within the chosen distance of the capture-wide longest path. It is not STA slack or a congestion measurement.

Register Q → D/control excludes external and constant launches, clock-to-Q and setup. Whole-cone timing includes all source boundaries. Both preserve the full selected cone and use its supplied static timing model. Actor, task and operation filters select work; function and delay filters select its circuits. The prepared viewer loads rendering indexes; the original native graph and records remain separately retained, hash-bound evidence. They need not all be loaded into the browser to render the scene.

Native timing path adds an independent pink static STA overlay selected in Chip & resources. Its ordered pins and delays come from a saved report bound to the same build and checkpoint. It does not establish activity or sensitization on a displayed clock, or a dependency on the selected operation. Public completion annotations likewise add no execution edges.
