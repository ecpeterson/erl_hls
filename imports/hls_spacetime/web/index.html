Distributed accesses are shown at their associated native tile positions by default. Filled write glyphs select known logical write-mask bits; hollow read glyphs show whole-word context because the consumed read-bit subset is unknown. Writes without bit identities also remain hollow context. Cells sharing a tile share a glyph. These marks do not show native switching or the logic computing a value. Turning off Distributed accesses restores aggregate symbols, whose centres are not computation locations. Drag the stack to rotate it. Select an operation or timeline tick, then follow its inputs or consumers.

Solid storage links join a write at C to Q at C+1; squares mark the new sample. Dashed links retain the same version between samples. By default, only versions with recorded uses are drawn, ending at their last use; a hold or delayed read response does not extend source-word use. Enable Full storage history to see all residence. Hidden does not mean proven dead: unobserved consumers may exist. Selecting a word expands its qualified cells by default; turn off Expand selected word into cells to group them. Other words remain grouped. Grey rails contain unknown or unplaced portions; their off-chip position is a layout convention. Residence depends on the declared write coverage. Expanded cells do not establish native write enables or exclusive task occupancy. The register example has 409 mapped bits and 128 unknown bits; its exact consumed read subset remains unknown.

Native timing path adds an independent pink static STA overlay to the stack and flat chip. Its ordered pins and delays come from a saved report bound to the same build and checkpoint. Path max/0 passes through 42 native cells and 40 logic levels: 8.808 ns data delay, comprising 4.075 ns logic and 4.733 ns routing. Lines join placed cells, not actual wire trajectories. The comparison layer does not establish activity or sensitization on that clock, or a dependency on the selected operation. Public completion annotations likewise create no execution edges.
