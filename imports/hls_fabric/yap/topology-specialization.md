## Dynamic collection contract

Capacity, membership captured when a collection opens, and the algebra are separate. A fixed-membership first implementation must not make membership permanently immutable. Inactive members supply the identity without necessarily occupying storage; active missing members still block completion. Empty membership completes according to the actor protocol. Associativity allows tree regrouping with fixed leaf order; arrival-order folding additionally requires commutativity. Failure does not erase outstanding contribution obligations. State queries must distinguish inactive, arrived, missing and early contributions.

### Exclusive early collection: revised protocol and experiment

The admission-only result motivates replacing opportunistic fallback with an explicit `exclusive_epoch` contract. Transfer tags belong to one state-independent, commutative member reduction. One bank binds to the first arrival's phase epoch, accepts inputs during callback ownership, validates the eventual open's key/mask, and releases only when completion is consumed. Bad keys, duplicates and malformed reserved traffic fail the actor rather than falling back after partial folding. Reducer failure still drains the required population. Different keys cannot interleave while the bank is incomplete. In the registered-boundary prototype a successor waits in its existing producer slot while a complete bank awaits release; there is no extra lookahead bank.

For this Snowflake protocol, a receiver consumes its transfer collection before emitting its merge-done acknowledgment; the global tree barrier waits for all vertices before authorizing subsequent transfer work. Local advancement skips intervening growth acknowledgments, not the merge barrier. Thus each receiver's consumption precedes every sender's next transfer epoch. This is a source-level design argument, **not a compiler-checked property or a formal proof of the implementation**. Reliable, exactly-once transport and correct participant identities remain assumptions.

Preregistered structural expectation: capture all 3,264 original consumed transfers and retain 1,152 completion visits, removing 2,112 visits before any change in the original 188 early postponements. Removing those too would give 8,469 instead of 10,769 total visits. Do not predict routed frequency from these counts: merge/open/admission now share scheduler logic. Require generated-function interleavings, real RAM ownership/reset/failure tests, unchanged original-decoder output obligations, and fewer visits before an expensive physical measurement.
