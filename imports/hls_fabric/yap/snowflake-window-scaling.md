## Measured interpretation

FF cost per vertex rises only 3.7% from D3/H3 to D5/H5, versus 26.1% for LUTs. Holding time depth at three still raises LUT/vertex by 21.2%. Increased interior connectivity is a concrete candidate: the D3 native hierarchy already assigns higher cost to bulk merge kernels with more neighbors. The [area accounting](../docs/artifacts.md#historical-reports) (`experiments/snowflake/hardware/scaling-area.md`) separates kernels from engine state/control; the latter is not a pure storage measurement.

The matched 96-sheet height controls have almost identical service, 29.25 versus 29.354 clocks, while differing substantially in area. This does not justify shortening the decoder's temporal buffer without accuracy evidence. The main three 0.5% cohorts hold at most 1/3/1 queued inputs and never backpressure their independent source; final H corrections remain in the window rather than being silently drained by extra quiet sheets.

The [conditional sharing screen](../docs/artifacts.md#historical-reports) (`experiments/snowflake/profiling/sharing-headroom.md`) adds one/two clocks to every body phase while retaining actual phase decisions, 50-clock offers and FIFO8. Registered seed-zero means 34.664/39.941 clocks are reproduced. All four finite cohorts retain zero external-source backlog; worst FIFO occupancy reaches four/six. This provides a service budget for a future area experiment, not a shared-hardware area or clock prediction. At 40-clock offers, corresponding to the qualified H3 clock of 25 ns, the conditional H5 replay has less margin: a six-clock body reaches FIFO7, and a seven-clock body fills FIFO8 and leaves four source-held sheets in one cohort. H5 physical closure remains separate. Prefer removing redundant operand copies without adding phase clocks before spending this thinner budget on sharing; banking or circular storage is still optional.

## Candidate: remove redundant operand copies

D5/H3's native direct engine uses 12,354 LUTs and 20,909 FFs, but snapshot copies are not separately attributed. In [local_probe.py](../experiments/snowflake/hardware/local_probe.py), old node/edge state changes only at reset or COMMIT; EXECUTE and GATHER consume it earlier. Replace wide self/neighbor/edge snapshots with stable views while keeping the phase sequence: DROP reads the node/edge above (top nodes use themselves; absent top edges are zero), while grow/merge read local nodes, actual neighbors and local edges. Give each kernel its static source instead of introducing a phase-controlled shared operand mux. Initially retain the small access-predicate registers to avoid adding edge-full comparison ahead of merge logic.

Keep accepted command/syndrome fields, pre-DROP retirement masks, proposal/transfer/gather state and retained results. A same-edge successor is accepted in RESPONSE, after the previous COMMIT; output stalls must preserve those result registers, and reset must still abort the phase. Gate any experiment on unchanged full phase states, retirements and 3/5-clock cadence, including stalled results and immediate successors, then measure native area and timing. Synthesis already shares/prunes many declared copies, and direct views change fanout; neither savings nor unchanged clock closure follows from the source-level argument. This is a specific cleanup candidate, not a commitment to banked storage or a formal equivalence proof.
