# Autonomous local-owner streaming gate

Registered before controller implementation. Preserve the d=3 surface geometry, 32-bit cluster IDs, Erlang/XLS vertex transitions and frozen local phase engine. Its native XC7Z030 screen completed at a setup-derived 15.764 ns (8 ns requested), with positive hold/pulse margins. The sampled tight paths are CID comparison/selection into proposals, not the busy reduction. That screen motivates a 20 ns integrated-controller closure target; it does not qualify that clock yet.

Build the missing sheet controller around the same engine: DROP, whole growth, whole merges until quiescent, half growth, half merges until quiescent. Reuse simultaneous result consumption and next-phase acceptance. Supply an eight-entry syndrome FIFO, one active sheet and one retained correction-output slot. Reserve correction capacity before drop, suppress warmup outputs, retain ordered empty corrections, and allow the current body's work to continue after its drop result even if the output is blocked. Use a saturating warmup count and wrapping opaque sheet tags. A kernel failure or exhausted 10,000-step merge budget stops new work and exposes a terminal diagnostic; recovery is outside this probe.

Expected cost: 23–27 clocks per quiet sheet, against 23 clocks for ideal consecutive phase commands; less than 1,000 added XC7 LUTs and 512 FFs over the matched local synthesis. The controller should not introduce an internal path longer than the existing comparison chain; native synthesis and routing must test that expectation. At 20 ns, one sheet per microsecond allows 50 clocks. The original-seed 0.5% phase census predicts 24.387 core clocks per sheet before controller overhead, leaving useful margin, though finite bursts and blocked outputs can still accumulate backlog.

Use independently timed arrivals at 50 cycles for the main 1 MHz scenario, 125 cycles for continuity, and an overloaded 16-cycle control. Keep 0.5% as the main noise rate, 0.2% sensitivity and 2.5% stress control. Count source offers separately from input-FIFO acceptance and work starts; a backpressured source retains offered sheets rather than silently discarding them or delaying the next nominal offer. Measure finite hardware FIFO occupancy and source backlog separately. Exercise long output stalls, recovery, simultaneous FIFO push/pop, stable held outputs and exact ordered retirement. Quiet tails are correctness drainage, not evidence of sustainable load.

Validate each autonomously selected phase and complete state against BEAM, including unrooting and multiple incoming XORs, without pausing the DUT for observation. Reuse the checked lower-noise cohorts. Compare raw and mapped RTL; retain accepted-cycle logs, exact inputs and netlist identities. Export the existing decoder-service-v1 and canonical/Perfetto profiles with true phase/state dependencies and separate observed resource order. Do not replace causal edges with whatever happened to execute previously.

Gate: promote only after the source is independent, no accepted input is lost, correction order/state agree, and integrated internal timing closes at the chosen clock. Report failing or unfinished timing honestly. A whole one-sector d=3 window remains a probe; it is not a distributed 4×2-qubit acquisition tile or a decoder-quality threshold claim. Do not change the general scheduler, public compiler, decoder algorithm or noise model in this experiment.

## Outcome

The controller meets the registered quiet-service and matched-area priors: 23 clocks per quiet sheet, +295 logic LUTs/+145 FFs, plus one RAM32M. Generated/mapped RTL agree on 7,589 complete phases; additional raw and interface-stub cases cover faults, exact merge budgets, rollover and reset. No source backlog occurs in either original-seed 512-sheet lower-noise cohort at 50-cycle arrivals. The overload and blocked-output controls fill the FIFO and retain external source backlog, as required.

The integrated native run closes internal timing at the chosen 20 ns, with +1.192 ns setup slack, +0.058 ns hold slack and +9.090 ns pulse-width slack. Its setup-derived period is 18.808 ns, versus 15.764 ns for the earlier externally commanded engine. The constraints differ (20 ns versus 8 ns), so this does not isolate the controller's timing cost or support a claim of unchanged routed frequency. The quantitative 50 MHz gate passes; inspect the recorded critical path before selecting the next optimization.
