# Commit/publication experiment

Registered against `a71e698`: remove downstream publication readiness from the metadata service by handing each ordered effect batch to a separate, two-slot owner. Preserve actor capacity, mailbox depth, scheduling order, external outboxes and reset lifetime. Reserve storage before retirement and expose no batch before its state write is accepted. Expect one or two additional clocks per diffusion round; retain the cut only if its routed-period gain improves period × cycles/step.

The implementation tried three boundaries. A distinct batch/commit rendezvous, and then deferring the batch until the fixed-latency write response, both serialized an extra publisher activation: the four-actor replay rose from 171.5 to 291.5417 cycles/step. The least costly form sent one already-committed batch after RAM request acceptance into a registered two-slot publisher. It preserved BEAM events under ordinary and stalled readiness, and directed one-/two-stage RTL tests preserved order, blocked payload stability, reservation conservation and reset flush. It still rose to 203.5417 cycles/step (+18.68%); stalled readiness measured 173.25→205.0417 (+18.35%). Effect-bearing handoffs are common enough that the registered boundary is application-visible even when the publisher sustains one batch per activation.

The final candidate mapped from 49,863→51,313 LUTs (+2.91%) and 38,295→41,081 FFs (+7.28%), with unchanged DSP and BRAM counts. One matched native openXC7 route improved 26.09→27.33 MHz (+4.75%), far below the 18.68% break-even. On that partial timing model, normal step time worsened 6.573→7.447 microseconds (+13.30%). One seed does not resolve small placement differences, and native timing omits qualified BRAM and registered-DSP arcs, but the margin is too large to justify more routes.

Decision: reject the separate registered publisher and retain direct metadata-service publication. The existing synchronous handoff does not expose an effect transaction before the accepted state-write edge becomes visible to another process. Revisit only with a boundary that adds no causal handoff cycle, or with a workload and matched physical result showing a clock gain above the measured cycle penalty.
