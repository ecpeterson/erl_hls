# Four-cell decoder on Zynq-7030

**The four-cell decoder maps to 33,818 core LUTs, but neither placement seed completed routing within a one-hour budget. No new clock estimate was obtained.** Current D3 also exceeds the Z-7030’s capacity under the pinned backend’s optimistic LUT-sharing bound.

This is a decoder-only capacity/timing probe on `xc7z030sbg485-1`, the TE0715-05-71C33-A's device and package. It uses a closed 2×1 phi grid in each plane: four phi actors and four replay actors, with one phi scheduler and one replay scheduler per plane. It excludes data/measurement actors, DMA, Ethernet and external debug hardware. The compile-only pins are not a board assignment; no bitstream is produced.

Application revision `3724231` uses two XLS stages, II=1, the unit delay model, unflopped inputs and flopped outputs. All measured decoder primitives survive assembly into the two-pin harness, on one global clock. Core and harness pass `check -assert` and `scc -expect 0`. The [JSON](z7030-decoder-2026-09-20.json) retains input hashes, every requested seed’s congestion history, tool-reported phase times and validation evidence.

## Routing attempts

Both seeds were placed, then stopped while routing. The budget includes packing, placement and routing; it was declared during the first attempt, before either produced a completed clock result. The second ran concurrently with the first after compilation ended.

| Seed | Completed routing iterations | Last overused wires | Placement/refinement | Outcome |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 19 | 1,147 | 14.66 min | One-hour budget exhausted |
| 2 | 4 | 9,255 | 14.55 min | One-hour budget exhausted |

Sample count: **two attempted, zero completed routes**. Best, mean, population variance and worst frequency are unavailable. Neither the placer’s provisional timing nor the pack-only timing field is a substitute for a completed route. A timeout does not prove that the four-cell netlist is unroutable.

**A future completed route would still provide only a partial-path estimate.** The backend omits sequential timing for all 44 RAMB18s and eight RAMB36s. Its 48 combinational DSPs use coarse delays; register checks use fixed 0.1 ns values; the shared Zynq timing tables do not calibrate the requested speed grade. These [coverage limits](../phi-timing.md#measurement-limits) prevent a deployed step-rate claim.

The placements required 9,503 and 9,536 post-placement cluster/cell relocations. These repairs and the long congestion tails justify a focused placement/routing investigation; they do not isolate its cause. The timing prediction remains untested. No critical path from an incomplete route is presented as a measurement.

These are single, unrenamed mappings; placement seeds share the same mapping. The earlier [four-cell population measurement](decoder-populations-2026-09-15.md) averaged 33,629 LUTs across two naming seeds, with the same FF/BRAM/DSP counts. This run does not establish an area change relative to that distribution.

The [last routed D3 result](d3-arbitration-2026-09-14.md) used `xc7z100ffg900-2` and 18 phi actors across six phi schedulers. Its three seeds gave 14.46, 11.89 and 12.62 MHz: best 14.46, mean 12.99, population variance 1.1693 MHz², worst 11.89. Compiler, standard library, RAM recipe and physical tools match; application revision, population, device, pins and excluded BRAM/DSP instances differ. This is historical context, not a matched performance comparison. The registered prediction was that the smaller population would ease congestion while shared scheduler control could remain critical.

D3 was mapped and packed, but not placed or routed. Packing produces 140,334 LUT cells, including 33,231 with six connected inputs. Under the pinned backend's [LUT-sharing check](https://github.com/openXC7/nextpnr-xilinx/blob/68aeeb39f92e39bfb239c7e4a44dd93451fc1889/xilinx/arch_place.cc#L567-L589), six-input LUTs cannot share a physical LUT with a second cell. Even granting perfect pairing to every remaining cell needs `33,231 + ceil(107,103 / 2) = 86,783` physical LUTs, exceeding 78,600. This optimistic bound applies to this netlist and backend, not every possible D3 implementation. The packer's combined 157,200 O5/O6 slots and its unplaced timing field do not establish a fit or clock.

## Behavioral checks and reproduction

Fresh BEAM execution agrees with RTL through step 32 under ready and independently stalled outputs, for both the four-cell population and D3. The four-cell profile emits 132 statuses; its tiny periodic graph cannot select nontrivial corrections because opposite neighbors coincide. D3 supplies that coverage, emitting 63 X and 64 Z corrections under the physical harness's variable readiness.

The measured harness takes 93.125 clocks/step for four cells and 182.583333 for D3. The latter matches the [September 15 reduction-failure behavior](d3-reduction-failures-2026-09-15.md), rather than the 175.958333 clocks/step preceding that change. The separate profile comparison's readiness pattern gives 93 / 93.041667 clocks/step for four cells. These cycle counts are deterministic workload observations, not frequency measurements.

Eleven timing-runner regressions pass, including real mapping/assembly, rectangular and single-plane simulation, and malformed-coordinate rejection. Nineteen source-contract tests pass, the source audit reports no new gaps, and Dialyzer succeeds.

Use the [Zynq-7030 commands](../phi-timing.md#zynq-7030-population-probe), then extend the same stage with `--phase route --seeds 1 2 --jobs 2`. The existing native tools and checked chip database are reused. This experiment enforced a one-hour limit on each nextpnr process and retained both interrupted logs. Only two physical seeds are requested; D3's capacity bound avoids an unnecessary full route. Full artifacts remain under `experiments/07-openxc7/build/decoder-timing-20260920/` in the saved checkout.
