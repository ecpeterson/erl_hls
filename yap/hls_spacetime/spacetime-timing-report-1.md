Every one has known endpoint timing; every branch has the `partial` routing disposition and intra-site attachment gaps. For example, `net/52/50` has 0.147 ns SDF delay and pin arrivals 0.816 → 0.963 ns. One admissible located branch exists, but extra site-pin→routing-node alternatives remain unresolved; they are not proven unused. The arrival renderer deliberately declines to interpolate those partial branches. This is unresolved intermediate geometry/height, not missing endpoint SDF timing. The shelf label now distinguishes timing from routing geometry.

The exporter retains connectivity and tile/PIP positions, not per-wire delay or complete metal centrelines. Better route correspondence can improve intermediate placement, but cannot turn chord-length interpolation into measured electrical propagation. No new extraction was attempted for this diagnosis.
