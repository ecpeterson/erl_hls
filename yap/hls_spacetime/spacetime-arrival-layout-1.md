# Within-clock arrival and structural urgency

The scene now unfolds combinational work through a clock interval rather than drawing every pin at the same height. Rendering algorithm 3 adds native pin/arc correspondence and per-pin latest-arrival bounds to the retained complete-path costs. Forward/backward passes reuse each acyclic cone; they do not enumerate paths. Unknown upstream timing remains unknown, and unknown downstream timing does not erase a known earlier arrival.

The recorder samples old Q, D and enables at C before rising edge C. Plane `B_C` is the relative old-Q launch reference; `B_(C+1)` is the boundary for write C and the following Q sample. Saved cycles stay unchanged. A common hypothetical display budget, initially 10 ns, maps arrival `a` to `C + a/budget`. Late paths extend beyond the boundary; no per-cone normalization hides an overrun. The initial three-clock window and increased spacing are defaults, not caps. Clock planes remains available for comparison.

Propagation ends at `A(from)+arc_delay`; a separate neutral readiness annotation joins that arrival to the latest reconvergent `A(to)`. Neutral root annotations reach the capture boundary, while red marks identify a hypothetical-budget overrun. Missing following samples do not create capture geometry. Recorded write glyphs move to the boundary independently of whether a qualified timed root exists; full-clock load glyphs are omitted in arrival mode, while sampled residence histories remain. Unknown time uses a labelled shelf; out-of-scope pins/arcs remain in the retained tables without acquiring an arrival height.

Native pin bounds are distinct from intermediate routed heights. Only continuous, resolved ordered chord chains receive interpolation. Each source-to-sink SDF net delay is distributed independently by chord length; shared fanout prefixes can consequently have different illustrative heights for different sinks. Missing or ambiguous geometry leaves intermediate heights unknown, retaining qualified pin and arc timing. These are schematic paths and conservative structural bounds, not per-wire delay measurements, switching wavefronts, STA slack, or a qualified clock period. Clock-to-Q, setup, skew, exceptions and sensitization remain outside these bounds.

Computation + urgency uses destination hue and lightness from distance below the capture-wide longest path in the same timing scope. The initial highlight bandwidth is 1 ns. This scale is independent of the hypothetical height budget. The local-delay table distinguishes native arc cost, complete through-path cost, relative margin, and from/via/latest arrivals. Only the inspection table is limited to its first 40 sorted rows; circuit geometry has no top-N truncation. Coincident arrival geometry retains contributors and distinct times at equal XY.
